About These Listings

Some properties are listed on the MLS, while others are offered through affordable housing programs and may not be publicly advertised. Availability, pricing, and eligibility guidelines are subject to change.

Joining the Interest List

Joining the Interest List does not guarantee eligibility or approval. It simply begins the process of being considered and matched with opportunities you may qualify for.

Income limits shown are for reference only and are based on 2025 HUD San Diego County data. Final eligibility is determined by the housing program administrator in accordance with program guidelines.
